# Shopify App Logo 2026 Reference

Snapshot verified: 2026-05-03.

Primary official sources:

- Shopify App Store best practices: https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/shopify-app-store/best-practices
- Shopify App Store requirements: https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/shopify-app-store/app-store-requirements
- Submit your app for review: https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/app-store-review/submit-app-for-review

## Required/Expected App Icon Standard

Shopify app icon specification:

- `1200px by 1200px`
- `JPEG` or `PNG`
- Keep corners square because Shopify rounds them automatically
- Include padding so the logo does not touch the edges
- Use bold colors and simple, recognizable patterns
- Avoid text, screenshots, and Shopify trademarks
- App icon in Dev Dashboard and app listing should be identical

## Related Feature Image Standard

Do not confuse app icons with feature images. Feature images use:

- `1600px by 900px`
- `16:9`
- Static image or short video for feature media
- Simple composition with one focal point
- Solid backgrounds with good contrast
- Alt text

Use `$shopify-feature-image` for feature media, not this skill.

## Listing Image Rejection Checklist

Reject or revise icons that include:

- Pricing information
- Statistics, rankings, data points, or guarantees
- Phrases like "the first", "the best", "the only", "#1", "most powerful"
- Reviews, testimonials, ratings, merchant quotes, or star ratings
- Shopify trademarks, Shopify logo, or Shopify bag mark
- Screenshots or miniature app UI
- Mostly text or app-name lettering
- Cropped marks that touch the edge or would be cut by rounded corners
- Low-contrast or highly detailed artwork that fails at small sizes

## Practical Quality Checklist

Before finalizing:

- The mark is recognizable at `48x48`.
- The mark still reads in grayscale.
- The silhouette is simple enough to identify quickly.
- The icon does not rely on tiny text or detailed UI.
- The icon matches the app's real product category and brand.
- The master asset is saved before producing smaller derivatives.
